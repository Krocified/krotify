## Execution order

1. Selection and keyboard (plan 1) — the shared selection model and the bulk add helper are consumed by plan 4.
2. Artwork and artists (plan 3) and Import (plan 2) in parallel.
3. Playlists (plan 4) after plan 1.
4. Player (plan 5) last: it is the largest surface change and benefits from the ambient lib and sleep caption being isolated first.
5. Device checks for plans 2, 3, 4 and 5; record them in `docs/research/v8-device-checks.md`.
